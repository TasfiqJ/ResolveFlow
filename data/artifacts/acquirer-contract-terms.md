# Acquirer contract service level terms (synthetic, restricted)

Owner: Vendor Management. Classification: restricted.

The primary acquirer agreement commits to 99.95 percent monthly authorization availability
measured at the acquirer edge, with service credits at 10 percent of monthly fees below 99.95
and 25 percent below 99.5. Credits are claimed within 30 days of the affected month.

Commercial terms, including per-transaction interchange-plus pricing and the volume tier
schedule, are confidential to Vendor Management and Finance. They must not be quoted to
merchants, in incident communications, or in support correspondence.

The secondary acquirer carries no availability commitment and is contracted as overflow capacity
only. Routing sustained production volume to the secondary path is a commercial decision, not an
engineering one.
